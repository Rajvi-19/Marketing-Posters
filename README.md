# Marketing Poster Design Collection

A collection of **marketing and promotional poster designs** created as part of my graphic design work. The collection includes five posters designed to communicate product offers, promotions, and marketing information through clear layouts and engaging visuals.

## 🎨 Project Overview

These posters focus on combining **visual appeal, product presentation, promotional messaging, and structured layouts** to create designs suitable for digital marketing and promotional campaigns.

The collection demonstrates different approaches to:

- Product-focused layouts
- Promotional offers and discounts
- Visual hierarchy
- Typography
- Product image placement
- Pricing and offer presentation
- Color and composition
- Call-to-action placement

## ✨ Design Approach

The posters were designed with emphasis on:

- **Visual hierarchy** — making important information easy to notice
- **Product focus** — keeping the featured products visually prominent
- **Readable typography** — maintaining clarity across headings, descriptions, and prices
- **Promotional elements** — using badges, labels, offers, and pricing to communicate marketing information
- **Balanced composition** — arranging text and visuals without overcrowding the layout
- **Consistent branding** — maintaining a cohesive visual identity within each design
